Your league stays yours

Nothing about your leagues, teams or players ever reaches us. The only thing we count is how the headset app is used, and you can switch that off.

This website

No server, no account, no cookies, no analytics. Your connection lives in this browser only.

Vision Pro app
Two counts

The website

These pages are plain files on GitHub Pages. Your Sleeper username, ESPN league id and team choice are saved in your browser's localStorage and read by nothing but these pages. Your browser talks directly to Sleeper and ESPN's public endpoints to load your league, and to Google Fonts for the typefaces; we never see any of it. Forget this browser on the board, or clearing your browser data, erases it.

The Apple Vision Pro app

The app reads your board from your own Mac on your home network, plays a video address you paste, and writes its summaries on the headset with Apple Intelligence. None of that leaves your network or reaches us.

Up to version 1.0 the app sends us nothing. From version 1.0.1 it sends a few usage counts, so we can see which parts of the app are used:

WhatDetails
session_startWhether you've opened the app before, and how many days since you last did.
space_openWhich space you opened: the board, the immersive board, the tabletop or the stadium.
Install idA random id made on your headset the first time a count is kept. Deleting the app resets it.
App factsThe app's version and that it runs on visionOS.

Never sent: your name, leagues, teams, managers or players, your board's address, any video address, anything you type, your location, or an advertising id. There's no tracking prompt because nothing tracks you, no ads, no session recording, and no third-party SDK.

Where it goes: the counts go to our own relay at mutaafaziz.com, which strips cookies and shortens your IP address before forwarding them to our analytics service, PostHog (US). No location is worked out from them. They are used only to improve the app, never sold, never shared with advertisers, and never combined with data from other companies.

Turning it off: open the gear, then Privacy, and switch off Share usage stats. That stops the counts and deletes the install id and anything not yet sent.

Children

Fantasy Edge is a fantasy football tool for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

Your requests

Because we hold no name, email or account, the counts can't be traced back to you. To ask about them, or to have the counts for your install id deleted, contact us through mutaafaziz.com/contact. The switch above deletes your id immediately on the headset.

Changes

If what the app collects ever changes, this page and the App Store's privacy details change in the same release.

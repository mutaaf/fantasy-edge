integration-11-perf: stadium performance pass (perf/integration-11 on immersive/quality @ c637059)

Measured in the visionOS 26.5 simulator (cloned "AVP perf-i11"), Debug build, with -stadiumStats,
on a machine running other agents' builds at the same time. Counts are exact. Timings are the
simulator's and carry that load: the ratios transfer, the absolute seconds need a Vision Pro.
Frame time was not measured and cannot be here (docs/ART_BIBLE.md, Budget).

After:  the same shots on this branch (after/stats-raw.txt, plus five seats of bowl-wide).

- **Budget:** 70k triangles, 20 parts, 50 MB. Every model is within it. Textures ship packed inside the models, about 11 MB unique.

## The contract every client shares

`scene.bowl.seating` is generated by `scene.py`, which is also the code the kit builds from.
- It holds every seat as `[firstArc, count]` runs along its row ring (`feet` offset, `pitch`), plus `sections` (id, from, to, side, vomitory) and `accessible` platforms.
- The total is 49,982 seats, about 47 KB of JSON.

`scene.bowl.mounts.rim` holds one headframe per rim bank: position, facing, pitch, headframe size, base, and `farSide`.

The tests in `tests/test_bowl.py` cover:
- rows and the tread under each seat
- no seat in an aisle, vomitory or tunnel
- sections tiling the ring
- accessible platforms
- seats facing inward
- the mounts
- a `.glb` twin for every model

## Iterations

1. **Slab bowl.** The procedural stepped slabs from the actor split. Treads read as a car park and the tiers read as walls.
2. **Blender kit, review renders** (`review/bowl_row16.jpg`, now superseded).
   - A 1,360-triangle chair, 49,982 instanced seats, and every aisle, vomitory and tunnel cut.
   - *Worst thing:* the review rig was 10× too hot and read as glare. At sane exposure it reads as a night bowl.
3. **Budgeted models** (`review/bowl_club_seat.jpg`, `bowl_club_foreground.jpg`, `bowl_wide.jpg`, `bowl_crowd_closeup.jpg`).
   - The full bowl as bands.
   - Foreground patches with real chairs and stairs.
   - The club and press box moved behind the ribbon fascia under a lip, so the ribbon is never hidden.
   - *Scores (club seat):* Read 4, Light 3 (review rig), Material 3, Scale 4, Life n/a, Clarity 4, Comfort 4, Cost 4.
   - *Worst thing:* seat bands between 9 and 30 yards read as a regular sawtooth, and the aisle stair tops are too pale against the treads.
4. **In the app** (`lookdev/s-bowl-wide.png`, `s-tabletop.png`, `s-field-level.png`).
   - `bowl-wide` from the upper deck reads as a real two-deck venue, with fascia, vomitories, tunnels and far bands under the Crowd actor's fans.
   - *Worst things:*
     - The upper preset's near patch leaves a black ledge along the bottom of the frame. The lip read as a wall from row 15 of the upper deck, so the near region needs rows below the wearer trimmed to the lip.
     - `field-level` came out as a flat blue and grey frame. The camera sat against geometry that is not yet identified: the wall glass or the Sideline boards. It is the next thing to chase.
     - `crowd-closeup` rendered only attachments: the simulator was starved at a load average of about 860, so this is not a finding.

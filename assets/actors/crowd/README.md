# Crowd kit

24 fans, generated by `tools/blender/crowd/build.py` (Blender 5.2, headless,
deterministic). Nothing here is hand-edited. Bodies, skins, suits, shoes, hair,
eyes and teeth are MakeHuman CC0 system assets assembled by MPFB 2.0.17
(`mh.py`); hats, scarves, props, poses and every texture the runtime reads are
built by the scripts beside it. Licences: `assets/LICENSES.md`.

Each fan is probed and scaled to the cast's height (MPFB's height macro is
gender-dependent and missed by up to 22 cm), hats are stretched onto the
measured cranium, scarves rest on the measured collar below the chin, props
are turned into the right wrist's frame, and seated legs are solved so the
ankles land on the tread after the runtime's chair lift (`mh.solve_legs`).

```
bash tools/blender/crowd/install_mpfb.sh && python3 tools/blender/crowd/fetch_mh_assets.py   # once
blender --background --python tools/blender/crowd/build.py                 # --scripted: the old procedural bodies
blender --background --python tools/blender/crowd/mh_review.py             # review: six MPFB fans, seated and cheering
blender --background --python tools/blender/crowd/kit_review.py -- sit     # review: the shipped LOD0, tinted
blender --background --python tools/blender/crowd/pad_atlas.py             # re-pad the impostor atlases alone
blender --background --python tools/blender/crowd/lineup.py       # review: the cast in rest pose
blender --background --python tools/blender/crowd/pose_review.py  # review: poses on four builds
```

`manifest.json` is the contract: skeleton, fans, clips, atlas layouts, tint
channels, LOD triangle counts. A renderer should read it, not this page.

## The cast

| Axis | Spread (24 fans) |
|---|---|
| Build | petite 5, average 5, heavy 4, broad 4, teen 4, slim 2; heights 1.45–1.92 m |
| Skin | 8 tones, light to deep, each worn by at least two fans |
| Top | jersey 6, hoodie 5, jacket 5, tee 4, pullover 4; about a quarter wear no club colour |
| Head | beanie 6, visor 5, cap backwards 5, cap 3, none 5; hair short, buzz, long, ponytail, curly, bald |
| Held | towel 5, foam finger 5, phone 5, blank sign 5, nothing 4 |
| Also | club scarves, face paint (stripes, cheek, half), sleeve stripes |

Club colour is never baked in. Clothing that takes a club colour carries a
0.86 luminance in the albedo, and the mask says which chip multiplies in:
**R** primary chip, **G** secondary, **B** face paint (primary).

## Files

| File | What | Budget |
|---|---|---|
| `fanNN.usdz` / `.glb` | skinned LOD0 (3000 tris) + LOD1 (900 tris), MakeHuman's rig, 10 clips | per fan |
| `lod{0,1,2,3}_poses.usdz` / `.glb` | every fan frozen in each of the 9 near poses, static, shading normals transferred from the full-resolution body | 29 / 10 / 3.6 / 2 MB |
| `fan_albedo.png` | mesh UV atlas, 2048², 6 x 4 fan cells, faces at 1.75x texel density | 16 MB RGBA in GPU |
| `fan_mask.png` | tint mask, 2048² (full size: a half mask left white wedges on near fans) | composed on load, not kept |
| `impostor_albedo.png` | 2560x2304: 8 fan blocks per row, 5 views x 6 poses, 64x128 px cells, each cell two neighbours at 0.503 m seat pitch | 23.6 MB RGBA |
| `impostor_mask.png` | 1280x1152 | composed on load, not kept |
| `impostor_normal.png` | 1280x1152, view space, +Z toward the viewer | 5.9 MB |
| `variation.png` | 256² per-seat phase, fan index (no shared 3-neighbours), tint jitter, pose bias | 0.3 MB |

Clips (24 fps): `idle_sway` (56f, loop), `sit` (hold), `stand` (16f), `clap` (12f, loop),
`cheer` (16f, loop), `fist_pump` (12f, loop), `towel_spin` (12f, loop), `wave` (32f),
`groan` (40f), `phone_raise` (44f). glTF carries one animation per clip; USD has one
timeline with the clips end to end, ranges in the manifest's `usdClips`.

Frozen poses: the six impostor poses plus `sit_look_l`, `sit_look_r` and `rise`, which only the
near rings draw (heads following the play, and the staged rise on a score). Which pose each fan
wears in a slot comes from `visual.crowd.nearMix`.

**Every pose file's fans face +Z**, measured after export and recorded in `manifest.json`
(`forward`). Blender's USD exporter with forward `Z` puts a fan's front on -Z, and the headset
then seats every near fan facing their own chair back; `tests/test_crowd_kit.py` and
`apple/verify_scene.swift` both fail on it now.

Impostor poses: `sit`, `sit_b`, `stand`, `clap_b`, `cheer_a`, `groan`. Views at yaw
0, 45, 90, 135, 180 (fan relative to the viewer, +90 facing the viewer's right);
225–315 are the mirrors of 135–45. Camera elevation 12°. A cell covers 1.2 x 2.4 m
with the pivot at bottom centre.

## Draw strategy

Fans move by group, never per fan on the CPU (docs/ART_BIBLE.md):

- **Near** (a few rows around the wearer): LOD0 pose meshes merged per group. A
  group swaps which merged pose mesh it shows.
- **Mid** (to ~12 yd): LOD1, then LOD2, then LOD3 pose meshes, the same way. The
  radius of that circle is bought in fans, not in detail: LOD3 costs 120 triangles
  against LOD2's 250, so the same budget reaches about 40% further, and what it
  gives up at 8-13 yd is a silhouette nobody reads that closely.
- **Far**: one quad per pair of neighbouring seats, cut from the view the pair
  is seen at, merged per section-aligned slice of the bowl in two interleaved
  variants. A group changes pose by moving its material's texture transform
  down one atlas row, and its edge is a section's aisle.

At the actor's rings (14 LOD0, 24 LOD1, 40 LOD2, 286 LOD3, the rest paired
cards) a sold-out bowl of about 50k seats draws 108k tris of mesh plus 2 tris
per pair of far seats - see `docs/actors/crowd.md` for what that measures at.

Axes: both exports are +Y up with fans facing +Z, metres, origin on the floor
under the pelvis. Seated, the pelvis sits 0.30 m behind and 0.52 m above the
origin (for a 1.75 m fan).

# Asset licences

Every file under `assets/` is original to this repository or CC0. Record each
third-party source here.

## Bowl (`assets/actors/bowl/`, built by `tools/blender/bowl/`)

The bowl's models are generated by script. Their textures are derived from
these CC0 1.0 scans, fetched at build time into `.work/bowl-sources/` (not
committed), then packed into each `.usdz` and `.glb`.

| Source | Author | Licence | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Concrete Floor Worn 001](https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001) | Poly Haven | CC0 1.0 | treads, stair tops, table trim |
| [Painted Concrete](https://polyhaven.com/a/painted_concrete) | Poly Haven | CC0 1.0 | risers, walls, fascia, parapet |
| [Metal Plate](https://polyhaven.com/a/metal_plate) | Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven | CC0 1.0 | steel trim band |
| [Leather White](https://polyhaven.com/a/leather_white) | Poly Haven | CC0 1.0 | wall and tunnel padding |

Seat plastic, seat bands, stair nosing, soffit panels and the lit interiors
(suites, press box, concourse glow) are generated with numpy in
`tools/blender/bowl/textures.py`: original, no third-party content.

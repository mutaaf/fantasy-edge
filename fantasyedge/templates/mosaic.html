Caveat. Leverage is φ(m/s)·σᵢ/s — the sensitivity of win probability to one player's remaining uncertainty. The same model sizes a league on the parent board and a player inside it. It assumes per-player outcomes are independent and normally distributed, which a QB and his own WR1 plainly are not, so a stacked pair is overweighted. Position variances are league-average, not player-specific. Read cell size as where to look, not as a projection.

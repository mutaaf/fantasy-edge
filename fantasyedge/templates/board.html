What the news signal is and is not. Headlines are real, dated and linked, pulled from ESPN's public feed. The arrow beside them is a keyword scan of that text - injury words down, opportunity words up - not a language model and not a judgement. Roughly two in three ranked players have recent coverage; the rest read QUIET, which means no article mentioned them, not that nothing happened. For an actual read, use Ask Claude, which reasons over the same real headlines plus your roster and never invents a story.

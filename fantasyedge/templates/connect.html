What this page will not do

Two things, and the reasons are not technical shyness.

It will never ask for espn_s2 or SWID

Those two cookies are your live ESPN session. Anyone holding them can read and change your ESPN account — not just look at your league. There is no input box for them on this page and there never will be.

A public web page that asks a stranger to paste a session cookie is teaching exactly the habit that phishing depends on, and it would be indistinguishable from a page built to steal one. This project's own rules forbid a credential ever touching the repository, and a page served from the repository is not an exception.

So a private ESPN league needs the app on your own machine, where the cookie stays in your own environment and never leaves it: python3 -m fantasyedge api. See the quickstart. That is not a lesser option — it is the whole app, with history, analytics and the draft board.

Yahoo cannot work here at all

Not "not yet". Yahoo's fantasy API requires OAuth, OAuth requires a client secret, and a static page has nowhere to keep a secret: every byte shipped to your browser is readable by everyone who loads it. Publishing the secret would let anyone impersonate this application, and Yahoo would revoke it within the day.

Making Yahoo work would take a deployed backend that holds the secret, performs the token exchange and stores a refresh token per user — a server, an account system and a privacy policy. That is a different product, not a missing feature.

So there is no Yahoo button. Locally, where a secret can live in your environment, Yahoo works today: python3 -m fantasyedge auth --provider yahoo --url.

Or just look at one

The demo board is one real person's real season, with the people removed: NFL players are public figures and keep their names, but every manager and league name has been replaced with a generic label before the page was built. It is not your data, it is not sample data, and nothing on it will ever become yours — it is there to show what a connected league looks like.

Open the demo board

Where your data goes: nowhere. This page has no server to send it to. Your username, league id and team choice live in this browser's localStorage and are read by nothing but this page. Clearing your browser data, or pressing Forget this browser, erases the connection completely. No analytics, no cookies, no account.

Every host this page contacts, and nothing else:
api.sleeper.app — your Sleeper leagues, rosters, live points
site.web.api.espn.com — the public NFL slate, scores and box scores
lm-api-reads.fantasy.espn.com — a public ESPN league, unauthenticated
fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com — typefaces

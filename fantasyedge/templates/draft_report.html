How to read this — and where it stops

The number is market disagreement, not skill. A positive score means you took a player earlier than the national ADP said you needed to. That is only a mistake if the market was right about him. Every good contrarian pick in history scores as a reach here.

ADP is where players go, not how they score. Nothing on this page knows a single 2026 fantasy point, because none have been played. This measures draft-day process against consensus, and it will look very different from a value review in December.

Kickers and defences distort the tails. They have almost no real ADP signal, so a K taken in round 8 posts a huge reach that means little — everyone must take one eventually, and the market barely ranks them. Two of the six biggest reaches are kickers. Read the skill-position picks first.

Ten teams, one draft. Fourteen picks per manager is a small sample, and the spread you see here is well within what one unusual run at a position can produce.
